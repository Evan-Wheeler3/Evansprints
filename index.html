Questions

Are minis legal for tournament play?

Minis are a standard way to mark your lie. Our standard minis are about 3 inches across. If you play sanctioned events, check the current PDGA rulebook for marker disc size limits.

Can I get a specific tag number?

Any number from 1 to 9999. League sets can be numbered in sequence. Just add a note at checkout.

How long does an order take?

Most orders are made and shipped within a week.

What if my engraving has a typo?

We send a proof for every engraved order before we cut it.
